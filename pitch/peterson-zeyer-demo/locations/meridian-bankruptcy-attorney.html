Chapter 7 vs. Chapter 13 in Meridian

The right bankruptcy chapter depends on your income, assets, and goals:

Chapter 7 Bankruptcy

Ideal for Meridian residents with lower income who want to eliminate most unsecured debts quickly. Most cases complete in 4-6 months.

Chapter 13 Bankruptcy

Perfect for Meridian homeowners who want to keep their house and catch up on missed mortgage payments through a 3-5 year plan.

Meridian Bankruptcy Process

Filing bankruptcy in Meridian follows these steps:

  1. Free Consultation: Meet with our attorneys to review your debts and options
  2. Document Gathering: Collect required financial documents with our guidance
  3. Petition Filing: Submit your case to the Idaho Bankruptcy Court
  4. Automatic Stay: Creditors must immediately stop collection activities
  5. Meeting of Creditors: Brief hearing with the bankruptcy trustee
  6. Discharge: Court eliminates qualifying debts permanently
